01Today

KB, document or analyst? No one records which — so we can't make the KB better.

Every PDLC run draws on all three — the Knowledge Base, documents in sources/, and the analyst's head — and leaves no record of which fact came from where. No map means no view of the mix, and no way to tell whether the KB improves over time.

risk

The analyst fixes the number in the chat; the KB still says the old value tomorrow. Without the mapping, no gap is ever fed back.

The point: chunk the Solution Intent into single claims, each carrying its source, so every one can be checked in step 4.

A claim is any statement of fact — a number, a date, a rule, a decision — that a reader could challenge with "says who?". Statements of intent ("this will help merchants…") are not claims. One fact per claim: "1.95% + $0.10" is two.

⬇
missing citationThis is how made-up content gets caught.

A sentence with no citation still becomes a claim. Step 4 searches the log, sources/ and the analyst's messages for it. If nothing backs it, it's Unsupported — and the analyst is asked to keep or remove it before publish.
