03WHY HERE FIRST

Why Interchange — and why Visa + Mastercard

A funnel, left to right. Of everything the networks send, two brands carry the book. Of the articles we actually implement, Interchange is ~18% and rising. And of the Interchange articles, 91.5% come from Visa + Mastercard — more concentrated than the book as a whole. Two networks, primarily one engine, one change type: the tightest first target we have.
