01What we do

Five components, each one an opening for AI

In Merchant Services, our Product, Payment Network Office and Technology teams collaborate on the Payment Brand Implementations (PBI) process to implement payment brand mandates across both Core and Commerce platforms. Each component offers opportunities for optimization and productivity through AI.

Core platform Commerce platform Visa · Mastercard · ~19 brands
1

Network Mandate Requirements Intake & Communications

Extract mandate requirements from the network portals, publish them internally, and communicate changes to merchants and the field.

2

Product Requirements Discovery & Prioritization

Create epics, estimate merchant, financial and systemic impact, and prioritize commitments against strategic objectives.

3

Development Life Cycle

Understand product requirements, create user stories, implement code changes, and test thoroughly.

4

Product Implementations & Monitoring

Technology change management, post-implementation validation to identify issues, and managing production challenges.

5

Custom Interchange Implementations

Interchange pricing programs negotiated between networks and top-tier merchants to deliver cost savings.
