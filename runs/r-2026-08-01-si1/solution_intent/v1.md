# Solution Intent — Discover Brand Routing (v1)

<!-- run: r-2026-08-01-si1 · domain: payment_brand · authored: v1 (code-blind) -->

## 1. Executive summary

*(Drafted last, from the body below.)*

Mastercard has issued brand implementation mandate **MCS-2026-R3**, binding on all
Mastercard-licensed acquirers including JPMC Merchant Services, with full compliance required by
**2026-09-30** [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L9–24]. The mandate
requires acquirers to support MDES token-based transactions end to end, to populate four new ISO
8583 DE 48 subelements, to revise interchange qualification around token assurance, to update
Banknet and Regulation II routing, and to obtain **Mastercard Acquirer Certification Level 2**
before the deadline.

Today the Merchant Routing Service identifies a card brand from the **PAN BIN range** at
authorization and dispatches to a per-brand handler
[src: context_set/confluence/discover_routing_kb.md L7–11]. That mechanism cannot carry a token
requestor identity, cannot express a token assurance level, and — as certification found during
the prior Discover work — cannot distinguish co-badged products
[src: context_set/jira/PBI-4471.md L57–62]. The mandate's explicit DE 48 subelements are what
close that gap.

A frame/source discrepancy was surfaced during authoring — the operator frame names this
initiative *Discover Brand Routing* while the governing source is a **Mastercard** mandate — and
was **dispositioned by the operator at GF** as an artifact of the fixture corpus used for this
run, with no scope change (`decisions.jsonl`, `scope_frame_mismatch`). The requirements below
therefore derive from MCS-2026-R3 as the stand-in source of record.

## 2. Problem statement

<!-- coverage: {1: source, 2: source, 3: source, 4: operator} -->

**Current state.** Brand identification is a **PAN BIN-range lookup performed at authorization
time**; once the brand is resolved the router dispatches to a brand-specific handler
(`mc_handler`, `visa_handler`, `discover_handler`), table-driven and reloadable without a deploy
[src: context_set/confluence/discover_routing_kb.md L7–11]
[src: context_set/confluence/discover_routing_kb.md L12–17]. On the message side, Mastercard
traffic uses **ISO 8583 (1987)** with brand-specific field 48 usage, with field-level mappings
held in a format registry [src: context_set/confluence/message_format_kb.md L6–10].

**What that cannot do.** A PAN range identifies an issuer range, not a payment credential's
provenance. It therefore cannot express: which wallet or device requested a token (DE 48.66), what
kind of wallet it was (DE 48.77), or how strongly the token is bound to the cardholder (DE 48.78)
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L19–37]
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L38–48]. It also cannot separate
co-badged products — certification on the prior Discover epic found co-badged Discover/Diners
cards **not distinguishable by PAN range alone**, accepted at the time as a known limitation with
the network signalling that an explicit brand indicator would follow
[src: context_set/jira/PBI-4471.md L57–62].

**Concrete consequences.** Three are stated by the sources rather than inferred:

- **Authorization declines.** Stripping or substituting a token before Banknet yields Response
  Code 14 declines from the issuer
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L42–54].
- **Interchange loss.** Failure to populate DE 48.78 assesses the **Standard** rate regardless of
  the transaction's actual token assurance, and **retroactive interchange adjustments are not
  processed** [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L115–129].
- **Decertification exposure.** Missing MAC Level 2 by 2026-09-30 downgrades **all** Mastercard
  and Maestro traffic to the Standard non-qualified tier and places JPMC on a 90-day remediation
  plan [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L81–104].

**Who feels it, and where.** Acquiring hosts and the merchant-facing decline engine (RC 55 now
means "Token Assurance Level insufficient", not "Incorrect PIN" — the wrong message drives
consumer friction Mastercard monitors via dispute-rate metrics)
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L72–80]; the settlement and
reporting path, via DSR format changes
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L163–171]; and Merchant Tech, QA and
Ops, who own the certification milestones
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L105–128].

**What it costs today.** No source in this corpus states current cost — the mandate states
penalties, not present position. Supplied by the operator at discovery *(illustrative figures for
this run)*: approximately **4.2M** Mastercard-branded authorizations per month through the routing
service, of which **~11%** now originate from a digital wallet; interchange assessed at Standard
where Token Enhanced would have qualified is estimated at **$180K–$240K per quarter**; co-badged
misidentification generates roughly **300** manual dispute investigations per quarter.

## 3. Client need & demand

<!-- coverage: {1: source, 2: source, 3: operator} -->

The demand is **externally imposed and non-negotiable**: MCS-2026-R3 is issued under the authority
of the Mastercard Rules and the Mastercard Security Rules and Procedures and is binding on all
licensed acquirers, JPMC Merchant Services among them
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L9–24]. It supersedes circular
MCS-2024-R7 in all matters relating to MDES token routing and interchange qualification.

Demand is dated, not open-ended: **2026-09-30** is the compliance deadline, with the network's own
certification gates falling before it
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L105–128].

Merchant-side demand signal: no merchant or client request is evidenced in this corpus; the
initiative is mandate-driven. Confirmed by the operator at discovery *(illustrative for this
run)*: two strategic merchants have separately asked for wallet-originated transactions to stop
being downgraded, which the same DE 48.78 work resolves — so merchant demand and mandate
compliance point at one change rather than competing for it.

## 4. Business objectives

<!-- coverage: {1: source, 2: source} -->

- **O1 — Achieve and hold MAC Level 2 certification by 2026-09-30**, avoiding the automatic
  downgrade of all Mastercard and Maestro traffic to the Standard non-qualified interchange tier
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L81–104].
- **O2 — Preserve interchange economics** by qualifying eligible token transactions for the
  Token Enhanced rate, which requires DE 48.78 to be populated at authorization; unpopulated means
  Standard rate with no retroactive remedy
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L115–129].
- **O3 — Keep authorization approval rates intact** through the change, by never de-tokenizing
  before Banknet (RC 14 exposure) and by handling the redefined RC 55 correctly
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L42–54]
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L72–80].
- **O4 — Meet the network's ongoing reporting obligations** so that the 60-day C3 production
  monitoring period passes without a remediation review
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L81–104].

## 6. High-level use case

<!-- coverage: {1: source, 2: source} -->

Today's flow is stated as: **authorization → brand resolution → handler dispatch → response
normalization → ledger write**, each step emitting a tracing span
[src: context_set/confluence/discover_routing_kb.md L18–22].

Under the mandate the same flow must additionally: carry the token requestor identity and wallet
indicator through brand resolution and dispatch without de-tokenizing
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L42–54]; evaluate token assurance
for interchange qualification before the rate is assessed
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L115–129]; select a Banknet endpoint
with a bounded timeout and STIP fallback rather than a host decline
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L63–71]; and emit the token coverage
data the network monitors [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L181–189].

## 8. Business requirements

<!-- coverage: {1: source, 2: source, 3: source} -->

#### R1 — Preserve MDES token integrity end to end

**Deliverable:** D1

The acquirer must never de-tokenize an MDES token before forwarding to Banknet, and must carry the
token requestor identity through the full authorization chain
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L42–54].

- R1.1 — MDES tokens MUST NOT be de-tokenized, stripped or substituted prior to forwarding the
  authorization request to Mastercard Banknet
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L42–54].
- R1.2 — The Token Requestor ID MUST be preserved in DE 48.66 through the full authorization
  chain
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L42–54].
- R1.3 — DE 48.77 (digital wallet indicator) MUST be populated for transactions originating from a
  digital wallet
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L42–54].

#### R2 — Populate the new DE 48 subelements to specification

**Deliverable:** D1

Four new data elements are introduced for ISO 8583:2003 authorization (MTI 0100/0110) and
financial request (0200/0210) messages, effective 2026-07-01
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L19–37]
[src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L38–48].

- R2.1 — DE 48.66 Token Requestor ID: N-11, conditional, required when DE 2 is an MDES token
  (BIN 520000–529999)
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L19–37].
- R2.2 — DE 48.77 Digital Wallet Indicator: AN-2, conditional, values 01–05
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L19–37].
- R2.3 — DE 48.78 Token Assurance Level: N-2, conditional, required when DE 2 is an MDES token; a
  value below 10 triggers RC 55 from the issuer
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L38–48].
- R2.4 — DE 48.79 Device Type: AN-2, optional, informational for fraud scoring
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L38–48].

#### R3 — Apply the contactless cardholder verification rules

**Deliverable:** D2

- R3.1 — For contactless transactions above USD 100.00 the terminal MUST request CVM results per
  EMV Contactless Kernel C-3 [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L55–62].
- R3.2 — CVM methods MUST be attempted in priority order: Online PIN, Offline Enciphered PIN,
  Signature
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L55–62].
- R3.3 — No-CVM is permitted **only** for transit and toll MCCs 4111, 4121, 4131 and 7523
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L55–62].
- R3.4 — The CVM result MUST appear in DE 55 Tag 9F34
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L55–62].

#### R4 — Bound the Banknet authorization timeout and fall back to STIP

**Deliverable:** D2

- R4.1 — If the primary Banknet endpoint does not respond within 4000ms the system MAY attempt the
  secondary endpoint [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L63–71].
- R4.2 — Any fallback attempt MUST be logged with reason code T in the transaction audit record
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L63–71].
- R4.3 — On secondary endpoint timeout the authorization MUST be forwarded to Mastercard STIP
  rather than declined at the acquiring host
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L63–71].

#### R5 — Re-map Response Code 55 and its merchant-facing message

**Deliverable:** D3

- R5.1 — RC 55 on MDES token transactions MUST be interpreted as "Token Assurance Level
  insufficient", not the legacy "Incorrect PIN"
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L72–80].
- R5.2 — Host systems and merchant-facing decline engines MUST display "Unable to process — please
  retry with chip card" for RC 55 on token transactions
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L72–80].
- R5.3 — Token transactions MUST be identifiable for this purpose by MDES token BIN range
  520000–529999
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L72–80].

#### R6 — Qualify interchange on token assurance

**Deliverable:** D3

- R6.1 — Interchange qualification logic MUST populate DE 48.78 wherever applicable so that
  eligible token transactions receive the Token Enhanced rate
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L115–129].
- R6.2 — The Token Enhanced column applies when DE 48.78 Token Assurance Level is at least 50
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L74–94].
- R6.3 — Revised tier rates MUST be applied from 2026-07-01 for MC World Elite, MC World, MC
  Standard, Debit Mastercard and Maestro
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L95–114].

#### R7 — Update Banknet and Regulation II routing

**Deliverable:** D2

- R7.1 — Banknet endpoint selection MUST follow the revised rules
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L132–141].
- R7.2 — Regulation II (Durbin) routing for Debit Mastercard MUST follow the revised unaffiliated
  network rules [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L142–151].
- R7.3 — MDES token routing MUST direct token BINs to the correct endpoint
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L152–160].

#### R8 — Meet the reporting and downstream data obligations

**Deliverable:** D4

- R8.1 — The Daily Settlement Report MUST adopt the revised format
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L163–171].
- R8.2 — The Regulation II quarterly compliance report MUST be produced and submitted
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L172–180].
- R8.3 — MDES token coverage reporting MUST supply the TRID population and DE 48.77 coverage
  metrics the network monitors
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L181–189].

#### R9 — Obtain MAC Level 2 certification through gates C1–C3

**Deliverable:** D5

- R9.1 — Gate C1: pass 100% of Category A and 85% of Category B MTS v8.4 test cases against a UAT
  environment connected to the Mastercard Simulator
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L81–104].
- R9.2 — Gate C2: submit 1,000 end-to-end traces including at least 300 MDES token, 100 fallback
  chip and 50 Regulation II debit routing samples, in JSONL via the Mastercard Testing API
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L81–104].
- R9.3 — Gate C3: sustain the 60-day production monitoring period without breaching the alert
  thresholds on token decline rate, TRID population or DE 48.77 coverage
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L81–104].

## 10. Constraints & design principles

<!-- coverage: {1: source, 2: source} -->

- **De-tokenization is not ours to do.** It is the exclusive responsibility of MDES at the Banknet
  gateway [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L42–54].
- **Routing must remain reloadable without a deploy** — the current table-driven property
  [src: context_set/confluence/discover_routing_kb.md L12–17].
- **Field-level mappings are registry-held**, so format changes are expected to land as registry
  entries rather than scattered code paths
  [src: context_set/confluence/message_format_kb.md L6–10].
- **Prepaid Mastercard is excluded** from MDES token requirements but remains subject to
  interchange and routing changes
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L25–39].
- **No retroactive remedy.** Interchange assessed at the Standard rate for want of DE 48.78 is not
  adjusted later [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L115–129].
- **DE 48.79 Device Type is not populated in this release** — optional and informational only;
  operator ruling at discovery, revisit if the network makes it conditional.
- **MC Standard sees no token-enhanced benefit** — its rates are identical in both columns, so
  qualification effort there yields no interchange gain
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L95–114].

## 13. Assumptions & risks

<!-- coverage: {1: source, 2: operator} -->

- **A1 — The format registry absorbs the DE 48 changes.** Assumed from the KB's statement that
  field-level mappings are registry-held
  [src: context_set/confluence/message_format_kb.md L6–10]. Not verified against code at v1.
- **A2 — Brand resolution remains PAN/BIN-based** until the explicit indicator is adopted
  [src: context_set/confluence/discover_routing_kb.md L7–11].
- **A4 — Which DE 48 subelements the current parser already builds is `[TBD — unsourced]`.** No
  document in this corpus states the existing subelement set, so the delta between what is built
  today and the four the mandate adds cannot be sized at v1. This is answerable from the code and
  is left for enrichment to fill.
- **RISK — Certification calendar is unforgiving.** C1 alone is estimated at 4 weeks and C3 is a
  fixed 60-day window; MAC Level 2 is revoked if remediation is not completed within 30 calendar
  days [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L81–104].
- **RISK — Interchange leakage is silent and permanent.** A missing DE 48.78 produces no error,
  only a worse rate, with no retroactive adjustment
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part2_2026.md L115–129].
- **RISK — RC 55 ambiguity is customer-facing.** Until the decline engine is updated, a token
  assurance failure is shown to merchants as a PIN error
  [src: context_set/sharepoint/mastercard_mandate_part1_2026.md L72–80].
- **A3 — Versioned ISO 8583 implementation.** The operator dispositioned `iso8583.c` as the live
  path at the onboarding gate, with `iso8583_v2.c` a fork built for the 2003 field set. This
  mandate targets ISO 8583:2003 DE 48, so the disposition and the mandate's target may disagree.
  Recorded in `decisions.jsonl`; **enrichment Arm 1 must confirm or contradict it.**
